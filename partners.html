The traveller's dilemma

Travellers love Bali, but many worry about Bali belly.

Before choosing where to eat, most travellers are quietly asking themselves the same three questions.

?

Is this place safe?

?

Have other travellers eaten here recently?

?

Will I feel good tomorrow?

Traditional reviews

Google reviews tell travellers if people liked the food.

Time2Eat

Time2Eat helps travellers understand whether other travellers felt good after eating there.

It's a new trust signal that sits alongside your existing reviews, not a replacement for them. Great food and a happy tummy are two different questions, and travellers want both answered.

How it works

Three steps, and nothing for you to manage

Your team carries on exactly as they do today. Everything happens on the traveller's phone.

1

Guests enjoy a meal

A traveller eats at your restaurant, just as they always would.

2

They scan your QR code

Your free Time2Eat code sits on the table or by the counter.

3

They share a quick report

A few taps on how the meal treated them, and they're done.

No long reviews. No ratings. Just real traveller signals.
