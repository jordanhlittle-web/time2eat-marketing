Why Time2Eat

Food safety data that's actually useful

Star ratings tell you if the food was good. We tell you if it made people sick.

Tummy Trust Score

Every restaurant gets a score.

Built from real traveler check-ins over the last seven days, not reviews from three years ago.

Tummy AI

One tap finds the safest places near you.

It weighs recent reports, Tummy Scores and distance, then ranks the spots actually worth eating at, so you never read the map yourself.

Live warnings

See the red flags before you order.

Recent illness reports surface the moment you open a place, so you can walk somewhere safer instead.

Pay it forward

A 60-second check-in protects the next traveler.

Tell us how you felt after your meal, and someone else skips three days in bed. The whole thing runs on that.

How it works

Three steps to eating safe

No accounts to set up, no forms to fill in. Open the map and go.

1

Check before you go

Open the map and see which places near you travelers trust. Green is safe, red is not.

2

Check in when you eat

Log the visit before your meal, or add one you've already had. Takes about a minute.

3

The score updates live

Every report feeds the Tummy Trust Score, so it gets sharper the more people use it.
