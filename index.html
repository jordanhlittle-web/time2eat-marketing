Why Time2Eat

Food safety data that's actually useful

Google Maps tells you a restaurant has 4 stars. Time2Eat tells you if someone got sick there last Tuesday.

Tummy Trust Score

Every restaurant gets a score based on real traveler check-ins from the past 7 days. Not reviews from years ago,what happened this week.

Warnings before you order

Recent illness reports surface instantly so you can pick somewhere safer before it's too late. No more finding out the hard way.

Pay it forward

A 60-second check-in after your meal could save the next traveler three miserable days in bed. The whole thing runs on that.

How it works

Three steps to eating safe

1

Check Before You Go

Open the map and see which restaurants near you are trusted by travellers. Green means safe, orange means caution, red means avoid. No guessing, no hoping for the best.

2

Check In or Log a Past Visit

Heading somewhere? Check in before you eat so the community knows you've been. Already been? Log your visit and report how you felt afterwards. Either way, your experience helps protect the next traveller.

3

The Score Updates in Real Time

Every check-in and report feeds the Tummy Trust Score. The more travellers contribute, the more accurate it gets. It's food safety built by the people who actually eat there.
